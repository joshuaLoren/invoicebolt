Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. Every feature — line items, tax, discounts, logo upload, and PDF download — is free with no sign-up, no watermark, and no limit on how many invoices you create.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no account system at all. Your details are saved privately in your own browser, so everything is pre-filled the next time you visit.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The invoice and the PDF are generated entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is ever sent to a server — check the network tab if you're curious.

What should I put on my invoice?

At minimum: your name and contact details, your client's, a unique invoice number, the date, a due date, line items with amounts, and how to pay you. Our guide on what to include on an invoice covers all nine essentials.

Can I save an invoice and edit it later?

Yes — your current invoice is automatically saved in your browser as you type. Come back any time on the same device and pick up where you left off.
